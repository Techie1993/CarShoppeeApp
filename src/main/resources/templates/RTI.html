Right To Information Act, 2005

Know Your Right to Information

The Right to Information (RTI) Act empowers every citizen to request information from public authorities, promoting transparency, accountability, and good governance.

Quick RTI Facts

  • ✔ Applicable to Government/Public Authorities
  • ✔ Citizens can seek official records and documents
  • ✔ Standard reply within 30 days
  • ✔ Appeals allowed if information is denied

RTI Highlights

Request Information

Citizens may request copies of government documents, records, circulars, reports and decisions.

Response Time

The Public Information Officer (PIO) generally responds within 30 days of receiving an RTI application.

Transparency

The RTI Act strengthens transparency and accountability in public administration.

RTI Process

1. Submit RTI Application

Submit an application to the Public Information Officer.

2. Processing

The concerned department verifies and processes the request.

3. Receive Response

Information is supplied or valid reasons for denial are provided.

4. Appeal

If dissatisfied, the applicant may file a First or Second Appeal.

Important Information
